# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-24

First public testing release.

- Filesystem-first browsing with virtualized masonry media tiles
- Image and video thumbnails with bounded asynchronous generation and caching
- Full-canvas Quick Look for images, animated GIFs, and video
- Keyboard-first navigation, marks, batch operations, and command palettes
- Search, sorting, hidden files, history, bookmarks, and fuzzy directory jumping
- Safe rename, copy, move, copy-path, open, and confirmed Move to Trash actions
- Persistent browser appearance and per-directory cursor/scroll state
- Native Omarchy themes and normal Hyprland tiled/windowed behavior

Version 1 is under active development and real-world testing. Patch releases may
follow quickly as more media collections, codecs, themes, and hardware are tested.
